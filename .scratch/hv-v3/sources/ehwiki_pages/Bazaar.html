Item Shop

Items marked in green are of unlimited supply; all other goods available at the bazaar were sold to it by players and thus are finite (up to 9,999). Items may be purchased in any number as a player's credits and the stock amounts allow (up to 99,999 per transaction).

Players with the Coupon Clipper Hath Perk make purchases at a 10% discount.

Note: If the "ACCEPT" button cannot be seen at the bottom of the page it means that the HentaiVerse window is too small. If the window size cannot be increased players must either scroll down (using the arrow keys) or zoom out (Ctrl + -) until it becomes visible.

The Market

The Market can be used to buy or sell items to other Players. To buy on The Market, you first need to have credits in your Market credit balance, which can be done either through depositing, or making sales. Credits in the Market balance can be withdrawn at any time, in any amount.

  • Buying:
    • Select the item you need - if it's not available, then no other player is willing to sell it at that moment.
    • Place a buy order: quantity and price per item.
    • If there is a sell order available at that price or at a lower price, you will get the item immediately. You pay the price of the sell order. This is paid out of your Market credit balance.
    • If there is no sell order available at that price or at a lower price, you won't get the items immediately. Your buy order will remain open until someone creates a sell order at that price (or lower).
  • Selling:
    • Select the item you want to sell - you need to have this item in your inventory.
    • Place a sell order: quantity and price per batch.
    • If there is a buy order available at that price or at a higher price, you will sell the item immediately. You receive the price of your sell order, minus 1% tax. This is added to your Market credit balance.
    • If there is no buy order available at that price or at a higher price, you won't sell the items immediately. Your sell order will remain open until someone creates a buy at that price (or higher).
  • Limits:
    • For items that are always available in the Item Shop (e.g. health Restoratives), you can't have open buy orders above the Item Shop price. You will effectively buy them from the Item Shop in the Market in that case.
    • Quantities are capped at 99,999,999 per item.
    • Buy order quantities will be reduced until disabled when the player runs out of market credit balance needed to make purchases.

The Armory

Allows the Player to Organize, Modify, Repair, Soulbind, Purchase, Sell, and Salvage equipment.

Monster Lab

Used to create and upgrade custom monsters that fight in battles and bring gifts to their owner.

The Shrine

Allows for the trading of artifacts and trophies for various rewards.

MoogleMail

Used for sending messages, credits, hath, items, and Equipment to other players in the HentaiVerse.

Lotteries

Awards various daily prizes at the cost of betting GP.
