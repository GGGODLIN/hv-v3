Leveling up occurs when a player earns enough EXP. Its occurrence will be indicated in the combat log.

Effects

Every Level

  • All Soulbound pieces of equipment will have their active stats increase based on Level Scaling.
    • This does not trigger until the player is out of combat.
  • An ability point is earned.
    • An additional ability point can be earned via Training.
  • The base proficiency cap increases by 1.
  • The range on the level of the monsters that can be fought increases by 1.
  • The cap on the power level of the monsters that can be fought increases by at least 1.
  • Spell costs increase by base_cost/100.

Formula

EXP required for next level = (current_level + 4) ^ (2.850263212287058 ^ (1 + (current_level+1) / 1000)) - (current_level + 3) ^ (2.850263212287058 ^ (1 + current_level / 1000))
