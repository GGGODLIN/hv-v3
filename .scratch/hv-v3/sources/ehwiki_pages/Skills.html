Innate Skills

These skills are available to all players.

Skill Status Effect

Defend 		You are defending from enemy blows. The amount of damage you take is reduced by 25%.
  • Defending lasts for 2 turns - the turn in which it was cast and the following turn.
  • 25% Overcharge (one whole yellow pip) is consumed if available to recover 25% base health.

Focus 		You are mentally prepared for casting a magical attack. The chance for your spell being evaded or resisted is reduced, but so is your chance to avoid attacks.
  • Focusing will leave the player unable to Evade/Block/Parry/Resist (-100% penalty). Monsters can still whiff at shadows.
    • Does not apply on the turn the skill is used.
  • Focusing lasts for 2 turns - the turn in which it was cast and the following turn.
  • Doubles Magic Accuracy. Against the monsters' Resist roll this is further doubled and increased by +100.
  • 25% Overcharge (one whole yellow pip) is consumed if available to recover 5% base mana.

Flee 		You are running away.
  • Fleeing may take a full turn to take effect, during which monsters may still attack.
    • The Yakety Sax Hath Perk makes fleeing instant.
  • Removes the player from the current battle series.
  • Counts as a victory for monsters involved. There is a cooldown on how often a player can count towards a monster's win stat.

Scan 		Retrieve data on the target.
  • Gives further details about a single monster, including its:
    • Health, Mana, and Spirit Points.
    • Class and Power Level.
    • Owner.
    • Melee attack damage type.
    • Damage type mitigation values.
    • Accuracy stat.
      • The percentage hit chance only takes the Player's Evade into account.
    • Avoidance stats.

Weapon Skills

A player's fighting style will grant additional fighting skills to players.

  • They cannot be parried or evaded.
  • If they are used while Spirit Stance is activated, the cost for Spirit Stance will be deducted before the skill is used. Thus, the skill will fail if the player doesn't have at least 10% Overcharge past the skill's cost.
  • Using skills will drain Overcharge according to the table below.
  • All skills have a cooldown time before they can be used again.
  • New skills are unlocked with higher Proficiencies. For Niten Ichiryu, the smaller of dual-wield and two-handed proficiencies is used for this calculation.
  • These skills use the player's current mainhand weapon's element / damage type unless otherwise stated.
Fighting Style Skill Cost Cooldown Prof. Required Description / Status Effect(s)
One-Handed
Shield Bash 		25 10 0
  • Does NOT require a shield.
  • Causes Stunned for 5 Ticks. Will not refresh an existing Stun.
  • Does crushing damage.

Vital Strike 		50 10 50
  • When used on a Stunned enemy, adds 90 stacks of Bleeding Wound to an existing bleed or inflicts 100 stacks.
  • Deals extra damage to a Stunned enemy.

Merciful Blow 		100 10 100 Deals damage, and if the target is affected by Bleeding Wound and below 25% health, instantly kills them.
Dual Wielding
Iris Strike 		50 5 0 Inflicts Blind for 20 Ticks.
  • This is increased by the Better Blind Ability.

Backstab 		50 5 50
  • Deals double damage to Blinded targets.
  • Has a ~50% chance to inflict Spreading Poison on a Blinded enemy for 20 Ticks.

Frenzied Blows 		75 10 100

Hits up to 5 targets for a total of 10-21 hits (depends on the number of enemies).

  • Against N enemies, each enemy will be hit between ceiling(10/N) and ceiling(20/N) times.
2-Handed Weapon

Great Cleave 		50 5 0 Does higher than normal damage and always crits.

Rending Blow 		50 5 50
  • Inflicts 3 stacks of Penetrated Armor on up to 5 targets.
  • Damage of the skill bypasses Monster Physical Mitigation.

Shatter Strike 		50 5 100 Inflicts Stunned for 5 Ticks on up to 5 targets if they have Penetrated Armor. Will not refresh an existing Stun.
Niten Ichiryu
Skyward Sword 		100 10 0
  • Inflicts both 3 stacks of Penetrated Armor (reduced to 25% mitigation) and adds 90 stacks of Bleeding Wound to an existing bleed or inflicts 100 stacks on up to 5 targets.
  • Damage of the skill bypasses Monster Physical Mitigation.
Staff
Concussive Strike 		50 10 0
  • Causes Stunned for 5 Ticks. Will not refresh an existing Stun.
  • Does magical damage, not physical.
  • Can proc Coalesced Mana.

Special Skills

Skills that have special requirements to unlock.

Skill Cost Cooldown Status Effect

Orbital Friendship Cannon 		200 50
  • Hits all targets.
  • Does Void damage.
  • Unlocked as long as the player owns at least 21 different collectables.
    • These must be kept in the player's inventory - not offered at the shrine. (It may take up to one day to become available.)
  • Damage greatly increased by unlocking the Manehattan Project Hath Perk.

FUS RO DAH 		100 10
  • Hits all targets.
  • Does Void damage.
  • Causes Stunned for 5 Ticks. Will not refresh an existing Stun.
  • Unlocked by obtaining and using the Dovahkiin or Ponyslayer title.
