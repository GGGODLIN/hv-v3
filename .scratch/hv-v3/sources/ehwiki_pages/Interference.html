Interference is a Compromise Statistic on equipment in the HentaiVerse mini-game.

Effects of Interference

Every point of interference will have the following effect:

  • Increases spell costs by 0.5%.

Getting Interference

  • A player gets interference from the equipment they wear.
    • All equipment other than staffs, cloth armor (except Ironsilk), and ethereal weapons have an interference stat.
    • The base stat average for a piece of equipment affects its interference stat.

Reducing Interference

  • Attach the Featherweight Charm to the equipment.
  • Use equipment with the "of the Battlecaster" or "of the arcanist" suffix, ethereal weapons, or cloth armor, which have significantly less or no interference stat.
  • Use equipment with the "of focus" suffix, which gives a Mana Conservation bonus to reduce the Mana Cost.
  • Perform Stat Fusion on Legendary or better equipment to raise its base stat average.
