## General

**Is playing the game needed to unlock anything in E-Hentai?**: 

  No. Playing the game is 100% optional.

**What is the best/fastest way to get credits?**: 

  The most efficient method is by playing the Arena. Credit gains can be improved by training Scavenger, Quartermaster, Archaeologist and selling any found artifacts or rare equipment and items to other players.

**Why does the HentaiVerse exist?**: 

  [COLOR:#a56b0d]"Boredom" - Tenboro, July 2014

**Does the game contain any hentai/adult material?**: 

  No; at most there is some NSFW language due to custom monster naming and abilities.

**What do all these acronyms mean?**: 

  Check here.

**Is the game free-to-play?**: 

  Yes; there are benefits to donating but no game content is pay-exclusive.

**Are there any limits on how much the game can be played?**: 

  Players are only limited by their Stamina.

## Spells

**I can spend ability points on abilities related to a spell, does this mean I have the spell?**: 

  No. Abilities are unlocked purely by level whilst spells are unlocked by either level and/or proficiency.

## Difficulty

**What are the advantages of playing on harder difficulties? What are the disadvantages of playing on lower difficulties?**: 

  Experience, credits, and the quality of item/equipment drops all improve. Drop rates are unaffected. The maximum power level of monsters a player may face increases which further improves EXP received.

## Equipment

**How do I link a piece of equipment?**: 

  Mouse over the piece and press C. This will generate a pop-up with the URL. Make sure Caps Lock is off and popups are enabled.

**Why can't I wear a piece of equipment?**: 

  Players cannot equip anything that is above their own level without soulfusing with it first.

**Why is my equipment not getting stronger when I level up?**: 

  Anything that isn't soulfused to a player will maintain its level until it is fused.

**Is there a way to undo soulfusion or make untradeable pieces tradeable?**: 

  No.

**I salvaged something by accident, can I get it back?**: 

  It will be available for purchase in the bazaar for 24 hours (and only visible to the player that salvaged it) but its purchase price will be based on a penalty cost.

**Can weapon proc chance increase as the player or weapon increases in level?**: 

  No.

**Can proc duration/effect increase as the player and weapon increase in level?**: 

  No.

## Monster Lab

**Can my monsters fight me in battle?**: 

  Yes, but your own monster won't give you gifts for defeating you.

**How do I know if my monster is active?**: 

  If it's at/above Power Level 25 then it's active. Note that at low PL it is very unlikely to score any kills.

**Do monsters have the stats that players have?**: 

  Yes, but they work slightly differently.

**What happens if I don't feed my monster?**: 

  Its morale will drain faster.

**What happens if my monster loses all morale?**: 

  It will stop bringing gifts.

### Crystals

**How do I get crystals?**: 

  Enemies drop crystals as they do with other items. Artifacts can also grant 5,000 of a random crystal type as part of their shrine reward.

**Is there any way to increase crystal gains?**: 

  Scavenger training will increase loot drop chance which will increase crystal gains. The Crystarium Hath Perks and HV difficulty setting boosts the number received when they drop. Fighting in the Grindfest battle mode for a high number of rounds also gives a drop multiplier.

**Is there any way to increase drops of any specific crystal(s)?**: 

  No.

**Is there any way to turn off crystal drops?**: 

  No.

**Do crystals have any use other than upgrading monsters?**: 

  No. But you can sell them in The Market.

## Artifacts

**I found a named artifact, is it any different from a Precursor Artifact?**: 

  Easter artifacts give fewer crystals and hath.

**Is there any way to choose the reward given by the Shrine for artifacts?**: 

  No.

## Item World

**What happens upon completion of an Item World?**: 

  A random charm is awarded, the equipment's stats are increased by 1% (2% for damage bonuses), and its maximum forge level is increased by 1.

**What effect does the quality of the equipment entered have on the Item World?**: 

  See these tables.

## Proficiencies

**Is there any way to increase proficiency gains?**: 

  Overall:  Only through training Assimilator.

  However, you can deliberately use spells to in each round to gain that particular proficiency.

## Tokens

**How do I get tokens?**: 

  Chaos and Blood Tokens are a reward for beating arenas and rarely dropped by enemies. Exchanging precursor Artefacts at the Shrine can give you Chaos Tokens. There is also a chance to win Chaos Tokens in the Lotteries. Soul Fragments are from clearing random encounters.

**Is there any way to increase Token drop rates?**: 

  The Tokenizer hath perk increases Token rates from monster drops. The Arena Token Bonus increases in higher Arena challenges.

**Is there any way to transfer Tokens to another player?**: 

  No.

**Is it possible to exchange Tokens of one type for another?**: 

  No.

**Is it possible to sell Tokens?**: 

  Soul Fragments sell for 1 credit, the others cannot be sold.

## Training

**Is there any way to reduce training costs?**: 

  No.

**Does training Quartermaster reduce your chance of getting items/artifacts? Does training Archaeologist reduce your chance of getting equipment/other items?**: 

  Technically yes but the reduction is infinitesimally small.

### Luck of the Draw

**Does it have any effect on equipment granted by the Shrine?**: 

  No.

**Does it have any effect on equipment suffixes?**: 

  No.
