## Update 91 Notice

"The Way Too Big And Very Overdue Equipment Update" has finally come to the HentaiVerse with update 91 (June 8th, 2026). Be aware that some pages still contain outdated information as we work to update the wiki.

The HentaiVerse is a browser-based RPG for E-Hentai found at hentaiverse.org. In the HentaiVerse players fight monsters in a variety of battle modes in order to gain EXP and level up as well as earn credits and various items. For more specific details see one of the links below or browse the HentaiVerse category. Current version: 0.91.

New players are encouraged to read the HentaiVerse FAQ and Advice pages.
