- # Overcharge

			
				From EHWiki
				
				
				Jump to navigationJump to search
				
				Overcharge is one of the stats in the HentaiVerse. In the Standard UI it is represented by yellow pips on the player's health bar, wherein each pip is equal to 25% Overcharge. In the Utalitarian UI it is a simple bar with the numeric value of current Overcharge.

## Uses

Players can activate their Spirit Stance with 50% or more Overcharge.

- Weapon & Special Skills require Overcharge.

## Gaining Overcharge

All players have a cap of 250% Overcharge.

- Successfully hitting an enemy with a physical attack fills the player's Overcharge bar by a 5-10%.

- A successful Counter-Attack will generate 5-10% Overcharge.

- A successful Offhand Strike will generate 5% Overcharge when Dualwielding and 3% when using Niten Ichiryu.

- Each successful Domino Strike will generate 1% Overcharge.
