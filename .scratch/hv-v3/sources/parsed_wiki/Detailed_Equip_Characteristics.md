# Quality

Quality directly determines how high the stats on an equipment can be (or low, in the case of Burden & Interference).

Some qualities have additional effects on the equipment characteristics, listed in the table below.

| Name | Base Stat Roll Range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crude | 0-40 | Never has a prefix. Never has a suffix. Drops Level Unassigned |
| Fair | 30-70 |  |
| Average | 60-100 | Can have a suffix |
| Superior | 90-130 | Guaranteed a suffix. Can have a prefix. |
| Exquisite | 120-160 | Assigned the player's level as soon as it drops. |
| Magnificent | 150-180 | Base drop chance = 0.0005% |
| Legendary | 170-200 | Guaranteed a prefix. Guaranteed all applicable attribute bonuses (except on shields, which roll 3 out of their 4 possibilities). Base drop chance = 0.00025% Can only drop on Hell and higher difficulties. |
| Peerless | 200-200 | Guaranteed all stats maxed. Can only drop on Hell and higher difficulties. Drop chance on Hell: 1 in 1,000,000 Drop chance on Nintendo: 1 in 333,333~ Drop chance on IWBTH: 1 in 200,000 Drop chance on PFUDOR: 1 in 100,000 A Legendary equip can be upgraded to Peerless through Stat Fusion. |
| Peerless+ | 201-249 | Attained by performing Stat Fusion on a Peerless equip while owning at least one tier of the Limit Breaker Hath perk. |
| Ultimate | 250-250 | Attained by upgrading all Base Stat Rolls on a Peerless+ equip through Stat Fusion while owning the maxed out Limit Breaker Hath Perk. |

Note: in older versions of Hentaiverse, equipment stats were rolled first, and then quality was determined from their average. This meant some equipment could have some very low stats. In modern Hentaiverse, quality is rolled first, and then stats are generated - guaranteeing they fall within a minimum and maximum range.

# Prefixes

Prefixes are modifiers which can be identified in an equipment's name between the quality and the material type (ie, Peerless Hallowed Oak Staff of Heimdall).

## Weapons and Staffs

Weapons and staffs can roll seven possible prefixes: the six elemental types, or ethereal.

Note that any one weapon can have up to two Elemental Strikes plus Void Strike.

Elemental weapons and staffs:

- Do not change the normal damage type of the weapon (ie, Slashing, Crushing or Piercing).

- Gain a Spell Damage bonus to their respective element.

- Gain an Elemental Strike effect in that element.

Ethereal weapons and staffs:

- Deal Void damage instead of the weapon's base type.

- Do not gain any Spell Damage bonus.

- Gain Void Strike.

| Prefix | Damage Type | Distribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ethereal | Void | 20% |
| Fiery | Fire | 15% |
| Arctic | Cold | 15% |
| Shocking | Elec | 15% |
| Tempestuous | Wind | 15% |
| Hallowed | Holy | 10% |
| Demonic | Dark | 10% |
