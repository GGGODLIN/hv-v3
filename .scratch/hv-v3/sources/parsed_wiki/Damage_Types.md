## Players & Monsters

Players can choose different damage types based on their weapon. Specific types always belong belong to it "parent" type, e.g. Crushing damage belongs to Physical and Fire damage belongs to Magical. In contrast, monsters do not work in same vein, e.g. Fire damage used by monster can be Physical.

## Slashing

- Axes

- Daggers

- Katanas

- Longswords

- Scythes

- Shortswords

- Swordchucks

- Wakizashis

Slashing weapons can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Bleeding Wound: A gushing wound is making this target take damage over time.

## Piercing

- Estocs

- Rapiers

Piercing weapons can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Penetrated Armor: The armor of this target has been breached, reducing its defenses.

## Crushing

- Clubs

- Great Maces

- Staffs

Crushing weapons (besides staffs) can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Stunned: A powerful blow has temporarily stunned this target.

## Void

Unlike other types there is no means of gaining specific mitigation against this type of damage (both for players and monsters).

- Ethereal weapons.

- Weapons with a Hollowforged or Voidseeker Charm attached.

- The Special Skills Orbital Friendship Cannon and FUS RO DAH.

## Fire

- Fiery Blast

- Inferno

- Flames of Loki

Does more damage on a specific  Day of the Week: Tuesday

Fire spells can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Searing Skin: The skin of the target has been scorched, inhibiting its attack damage. Cold resistance is lowered.

## Cold

- Freeze

- Blizzard

- Fimbulvetr

Does more damage on a specific  Day of the Week: Wednesday

Cold spells can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Freezing Limbs: The limbs of the target have been frozen, causing slower movement. Wind resistance is lowered.

## Wind

- Gale

- Downburst

- Storms of Njord

Does more damage on a specific  Day of the Week: Thursday

Wind spells can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Turbulent Air: The air around the target has been upset, blowing up dust and increasing its miss chance. Elec resistance is lowered.

## Elec

- Shockblast

- Chained Lightning

- Wrath of Thor

Does more damage on a specific  Day of the Week: Saturday

Elec spells can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Deep Burns: Internal damage causes slower reactions and lowers evade and resist chance. Fire resistance is lowered.

## Holy

- Smite

- Banishment

- Paradise Lost

Does more damage on a specific  Day of the Week: Sunday

Holy spells can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Breached Defense: The holy attack has penetrated the target defenses, making it take more damage. Dark resistance is lowered.

## Dark

- Corruption

- Disintegrate

- Ragnarok

Does more damage on a specific  Day of the Week: Monday

Dark spells can proc the following status effect on targeted monsters:

- Blunted Attack: The decaying effects of the spell has blunted the target's offenses, making it deal less damage. Holy resistance is lowered.
