- # Secret Pony Level

			
				From EHWiki
				
				
				Jump to navigationJump to search
				
				Secret Pony Level is the level 500 arena.

EXP Mod: 5.0

- First time clear bonus: 50,000c

- Repeat clear bonus: 1,000c

- Equipment clear bonus: 3x Tier 7

- Set to PFUDOR difficulty regardless of the current difficulty setting upon entering.

- All monsters have +100% Damage

- Normal player monsters have +100% Health

- Grants the Ponyslayer Title
