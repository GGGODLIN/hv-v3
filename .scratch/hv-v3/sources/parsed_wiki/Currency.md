- # Currency

			
				From EHWiki
				
				
				Jump to navigationJump to search
				
				The various mediums of exchange on E-Hentai are mostly generated by contributing to the site in some form or another. They are used to unlock various gallery and Hentaiverse perks.

## Types

| Type | Primary Source | Primary Use |

| Gallery Points | Uploading galleries | Downloading archives |

| Credits | Hentaiverse | Hentaiverse or exchanging for other currencies |

| Hath | Hentai@Home | Hath Perks |

## Trading

 For legal reasons the trading of E-Hentai currencies or accounts to 3rd parties in exchange for real money (or other currencies from outside of the site) is strictly prohibited. See Trading for Coins for accepted trading.
