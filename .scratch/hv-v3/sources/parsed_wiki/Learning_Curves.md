- # Learning Curves

			
				From EHWiki
				
				
				Jump to navigationJump to search
				
				Learning Curves is the level 10 arena. It becomes unavailable at level 150 when New Wings is unlocked.

EXP Mod: 1.0

- First time clear bonus: 1,000c

- Repeat clear bonus: 200c
