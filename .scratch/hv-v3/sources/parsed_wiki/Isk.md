Isekai Credits (often abbreviated as 'ISK') is a currency used specifically for the HentaiVerse Isekai Mode. Unlike the other currencies, ISK cannot be sent directly to other Players, used in the Gallery Point Exchange, used in the Hath Exchange, or used to purchase Archives.

## Sources

There are several ways to gain ISK, each limited to the HentaiVerse Isekai Mode:

- Killing monsters in the HentaiVerse
~20% chance per monster; amount modified by difficulty setting and battle mode, not affected by Player levels.

- Clearing Arena, Ring of Blood, and Grindfest Battle Modes

- Sent CoD MoogleMails that have been paid by the recipient.

- Transferred from the previous Isekai season, capped to 1,000,000 ISK.
This process is automatic, and ISK transferred to the next season takes priority over being converted to Persistent Credits.

## Uses

All uses of ISK are limited to the HentaiVerse Isekai Mode:

- Purchasing Items or Equipment from the Bazaar.

- Paying for CoD MoogleMails

## Rules

- Any transaction involving ISK may not also involve any persistent item, equipment, or service. ISK may only be traded for Isekai Equipment, Items, or services. Further rules on ISK trading and Isekai can be found here.
