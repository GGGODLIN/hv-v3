# Intro

This page is an unofficial bestiary of player monster lab creations above power level 250. Due to the nature of the data, chances are this page may not be up-to-date. Empty columns are for future patch additions.

## Raison d'etre

Why another bestiary?

 Simplified data entry to encourage updaters; removal of redundant/unnecessary data.
 Physical weaknesses/resistances (crushing/slashing/piercing) are same across monster classes.
- Magical weaknesses/resistances better represented generally through chart form.
- Monster skill attacks are all identical, attack names can be changed on a whim.
- Melee attack type of questionable gameplay value.
- Last scanned field removed to simplify data entry.

- Data gathering on custom high-level monster distribution to better inform players (gear choices / playstyle / monster creation etc).
 Current minimum limit = PL 250.
- Monsters above PL 423 (ie: those unlocked at player level 200) are highlighted.

- Help update add-ons.
 HV Monster Sensor, sample script (09/01/12)
