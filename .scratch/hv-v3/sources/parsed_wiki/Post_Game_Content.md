				From EHWiki

				Post Game Content is the level 400 arena.

EXP Mod: 4.0

- First time clear bonus: 50,000c

- Repeat clear bonus: 1,000c

- Equipment clear bonus: 2x Tier 7

- Set to PFUDOR difficulty regardless of the current difficulty setting upon entering.

- All monsters have +50% Damage

- Normal player monsters have +50% Health
