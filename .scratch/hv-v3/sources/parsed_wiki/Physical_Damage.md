- # Physical Damage

			
				From EHWiki
				
				
				Jump to navigationJump to search
				
				The physical damage formula for normal player attacks is: 

Physical Damage = attack_base_damage * (1 + hath_bonus) * (1 + tower_bonus) * (1 + crit_mod) * (1 + heartseeker_bonus) * (1 + spirit_stance_bonus)

| Factor | Description |

| attack_base_damage | Attack damage from all sources, including attributes, equipment and abilities. |

| hath_bonus | 10-50% from the "Dæmon Duality" Hath Perk series |

| tower_bonus | 0.1% per Tower floor cleared in the previous season of isekai (Persistent only) |

| crit_mod | Critical damage bonus (if critical hit) - 50% base, plus equipment bonuses |

| heartseeker_bonus | 25% if Heartseeker is active |

| spirit_stance_bonus | 100% if Spirit Stance is active |

This roll will then be further modified by mitigation parameters

## Not Taken Into Account

Infusions / Elemental Weapons

- Items such as Flower Vase

- Offhand damage

- Mitigations
