Personas are sets of player-made assignments for various character facets and settings. They can be freely switched around, edited, renamed, or cloned via the Character menu above the Primary Attributes area. This can be useful for players wanting to use or try vastly different fighting styles.

## Slots

Players begin with 3 persona slots. Additional slots can be obtained via Dissociation training up to a maximum of 10 at level 500.

## Separations

Each persona can have assignments for the following:

Primary Attributes

- Equipment Sets

- Abilities

- Difficulty/Font settings

- Battle Items

- Quickbar Slots

- Auto-casts

Everything else is shared between all personas.
