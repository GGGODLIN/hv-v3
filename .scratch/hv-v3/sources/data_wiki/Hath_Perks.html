Hath Perks

From EHWiki
Jump to navigationJump to search

Hath Perks are various augmentations to a user's experience on the site. These perks can only be purchased with Hath. The effects of perks are immediate (upon account refresh), permanent, and cannot be refunded once purchased except for the donator perks1.
